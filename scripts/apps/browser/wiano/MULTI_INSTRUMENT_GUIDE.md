# 🎼 Multi-Instrument MIDI Support

## ✅ What's New

Your Wiano piano app now supports **full multi-track MIDI files** with all instruments!

### Before:
- ❌ Only played one track (melody)
- ❌ Only piano sound
- ❌ Lost all other instruments

### After:
- ✅ Plays **all tracks simultaneously**
- ✅ Supports **128 MIDI instruments**
- ✅ Full orchestral arrangements
- ✅ Multi-instrument compositions

## 🎵 How It Works

### MIDI Upload:
1. Click **"🎼 Sheet Music"**
2. Go to **"MIDI Upload"** tab
3. Choose any `.mid` file
4. **All tracks load automatically!**

### What You'll See:
```
✓ filename.mid
Tracks: 4 | Instruments: 3 | Notes: 1250 | Duration: 120s | BPM: 120
🎼 Multi-track MIDI with full instrumentation!
```

### Playback:
- Piano parts → Use your loaded piano sound
- Other instruments → Synthesized automatically
- All tracks play together
- Perfect timing and sync

---

## 🎼 Technical Details

### Instrument Mapping:
- **Instruments 0-7** (Pianos) → Use Wiano's piano sampler
- **Instruments 8-127** (All others) → Use polyphonic synthesizer

### What Gets Preserved:
✅ Note timing (exact milliseconds)
✅ Velocity (dynamics)
✅ Duration (note length)
✅ Instrument assignments
✅ Track names
✅ Tempo/BPM
✅ Multiple simultaneous notes (polyphony)

## 💡 Tips

### For Best Results:
1. **Piano-focused files** will sound best (uses your quality samples)
2. **Multi-instrument files** work great but use synthesis
3. **Complex orchestral** pieces will have all parts
4. **MIDI karaoke files** will play complete backing tracks

### Recommendations:
- Find MIDIs with good piano parts (uses your selected piano type!)
- Try classical piano + orchestra arrangements
- Video game soundtracks work amazingly
- Jazz and blues combos sound great

### Where to Find Multi-Instrument MIDIs:
- **MuseScore**: Full orchestral scores
- **BitMidi**: Classic multi-track songs
- **FreeMIDI**: Public domain arrangements
- **VGMusic**: Video game soundtracks
